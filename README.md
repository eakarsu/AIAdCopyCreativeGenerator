# AI Ad Copy & Creative Generator

Run `./start.sh` after configuring the existing `.env` and installing dependencies with `./scripts/bootstrap.sh`. Startup checks ports, applies checksum-guarded additive migrations, verifies the saved configured accounts without resetting them, and adds development-only fictional training drafts idempotently. Existing records remain intact.

The Dashboard exposes the 15 generation features, advanced native copy tools, Campaigns, Brand Profiles, saved A/B drafts, Operations, and Usage. The AI reports button opens 14 additional report workspaces. Together these expose 42 supported AI tools. Every editable AI form has three complete fill-only examples and a separate **Generate with OpenRouter** action. Real saved sources are loaded through authenticated native APIs; examples never invent source IDs or approvals.

Native member sign-in uses the configured SEED_ADMIN pair, falling back to ADMIN and then PROVISION_ADMIN. Creative Governance Review has its own runtime account boundary, using PROVISION_ADMIN and then ADMIN. Development-only **Fill credentials** and one-click **Log In as Demo** respect those separate contracts.

Configure OPENROUTER_API_KEY, OPENROUTER_MODEL, and the native OPENROUTER_BASE_URL (`https://openrouter.ai/api/v1`). Provider failures and cancellation do not create successful results. Reports render readable sections, cards and tables and support readable Copy/Download. Saved native records open centered Edit/Delete/Cancel controls; saved copy also retains expiring native Share Links.

The 19 native domain collections have at least 15 configured-account-readable drafts. Training rows are explicitly fictional, manually authored and unreviewed. They are not provider responses, measured campaign results, rights evidence or publication approvals. Genuine report/score/receipt/audit histories remain genuine and are not filled with fabricated examples.

External delivery and scheduled competitor workers are not bundled. Integration screens retain their actual credential and worker requirements. The native publication evidence/approval workflow is preserved; sharing or generating a draft does not authorize publication. Historical unmounted prototype routes stay unmounted.
